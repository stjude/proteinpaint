Features:
- The genome-wide DMR scan is an element class of the differential-methylation volcano: pick "DMRs
  called de novo (genome scan)" and every called DMR is a dot, with the same p-value table, hover,
  download and dot menu as any other class. MMRF offers only the scan (queries.dnaMethylation.scanOnly).
- A scan draws two figures beside the volcano, on one x axis: a Manhattan plot of every kept DMR,
  hyper above a zero line and hypo below, height -log10(q); and the methylome-wide profile, mean
  beta per group in 100 kb bins drawn as the per-bin difference. The bin width follows Zhou 2018
  (Nat Genet 50:591), which bins genome-wide methylation at 100 kb; unlike that paper the profile
  plots a two-group difference rather than each group's level, and averages every CpG in a bin
  rather than only solo-WCGWs. It answers what a DMR list cannot -- on MMRF NSD2 only 0.9% of
  28,994 bins moved beyond 0.10 beta, median +0.0088.
- Both figures put the top 1,000 per direction on a hover-and-click layer. A profile bin reports its
  coordinates, its Δβ, each group's mean beta and the CpGs those rest on, and opens a genome browser
  on the bin. "Profile bin width" redraws the bins at 100 kb, 500 kb, 1 Mb or 5 Mb, averaging
  neighbours weighted by CpG count (28,994 dots to 609): display only, applied after the cache, with
  the statistics staying on the 100 kb bins the metric is defined at.
- Hovering a DMR or bin on any of the scan's three figures rings what overlaps it on the other two:
  the same DMR on the volcano and the Manhattan, the bin containing it on the profile. Only
  interactive dots can be ringed.
- The genome browser opened from a scan keeps the scan's DMRs as a track wherever the reader goes,
  including a position typed into the search box on another chromosome, with a hyper/hypo legend
  counting the DMRs in view.
- DMRcate's lambda, C and per-CpG FDR cutoff are exposed in the volcano controls for a scan, with
  descriptions of how each shapes DMR width and number. Each refits the scan; defaults are unchanged
  and keep existing cache entries.
- "Genes, expression and literature" on a scan links every DMR to the genes it touches by
  strand-aware TSS (promoter: within 2 kb of the TSS; body: the rest of the gene), reads each link
  against differential expression on the samples with methylation (promoter: opposite sign is
  concordant; body: same sign), and fetches PubMed articles with DOIs for a gene and mechanism on
  click, optionally within a disease (new termdb/dmrGeneLink, termdb/dmrLiterature).
- Clicking a DMR opens a violin of that region per sample, or a genome browser on the DMR with the
  scan's own DMRs as a track (new termdb/dmrScanTrack, read from the scan's cache), beside the genes,
  a new ENCODE cCRE track and the dataset's mutations.
- Three expression follow-ups, on the samples the methylation was measured on rather than everyone
  with RNA. New termdb/dmrGeneDE asks whether genes losing gene-body methylation are expressed
  lower, within log2 gene-length strata with a lower-tailed permutation null, on the genes whose body
  each hypomethylated DMR overlaps. It runs on either reading of a scan: the correction adds the
  background gate rather than being a precondition for the gene set.
  New termdb/geneBodyMeth measures Δβ over every gene body binned by expression change, the
  discriminating test for transcription-coupled deposition. GSEA ranks genes by mean gene-body Δβ,
  since a scan's rows are DMRs and not genes.
- Scan controls: chromosome, background correction, and "Min CpGs per DMR" (default 5; 2-CpG calls
  carry the largest effect sizes and split hyper/hypo like a coin flip). Statistics carries the
  whole-scan facts, including each group's genome-wide mean beta and the shift, and what the run
  cost in memory, CPU and wall time.

Fixes:
- GSEA on a scan ranks the chromosomes the scan ran on. It ranked the whole genome regardless, so a
  single-chromosome scan got an enrichment for a different contrast under a header saying otherwise.
- Differential methylation works on a dataset whose only methylation backing is a CpG matrix; the
  config advertised the scan for one, but the group picker's sample counts resolved 'promoter'.
- GSEA's 500-gene ceiling applies only to a scan's gene-body ranking. It had been lowered from
  20,000 for every caller, dropping large GO and Reactome sets from unrelated analyses.
- A proteome DAP volcano calls its single p-value an FDR again. The label is read off the term type,
  which was assigned after the label was computed, so every DAP axis and column said "adjusted
  p-value" for data that is an FDR.
- Hardening on the new routes: a caller-supplied chromosome list is bounded and deduplicated before
  anything fans out from it (one chromosome named 5,000 times was 5,000 full-matrix fits that never
  hit the cache), the requested gene set of termdb/dmrGeneDE is capped, and termdb/dmrScanTrack
  requires a dslabel so the auth gatekeeper runs.
