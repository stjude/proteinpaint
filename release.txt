Features:
- Methylation track in the genome browser: per-sample DNA methylation over the view range, with samples as rows and bins of equal CpG count as columns linked to their genomic position. The bins shrink on zooming in, down to single CpGs. With a gene, each bin is correlated with the gene's expression, and samples can be sorted by expression, group or mean methylation. With two groups, it shows the mean methylation of each and their difference. Clicking a bin shows the gene's expression split by that bin's methylation. Opened from a DE or DM volcano dot, for datasets with CpG-level methylation.
- Methylation vs expression starburst: one point per gene, plotting the core-promoter and gene-body methylation change against the expression change between a volcano's two groups. With a gene set group selected, it also shows the gene sets enriched in expression, in gene-body methylation, and in the two merged when they move in the same direction. Opened from the DM volcano with "Methylation vs expression by gene".
