Fixes:
- PDF download: characters outside jsPDF's built-in font encoding are spelled out instead of printing as mojibake -- a bin label "Methylated (beta >= 0.5)" came out as `"e0.5`. Covers >=, <=, the unicode minus, curly quotes, subscript digits and the greek used in axis labels (delta-beta, rho). SVG downloads are unaffected and keep the real glyphs.
- A categorical term served from a dataset's cached per-sample values (ds.termid2sample2value, e.g. MMRF's Methylator phenotype) no longer fails with "unknown tw.q.type when q.mode is discrete" when opened from the dictionary; only numeric cached terms are binned.
- DMR scan: with the background correction on, the volcano is drawn on the excess over matched background rather than the raw delta-beta. The axis label, the |Δβ| cutoff, the hyper:hypo counts, the dot colours, both hover paths, the Manhattan plot's sides and the gene-body loss set now all read the same column as the corrected p-value. Previously the figure paired a corrected p with an uncorrected effect size, and the cutoff gated the two directions unequally on any cohort whose methylome has shifted.
- DMR scan: the background p is labelled "unadjusted p vs matched background" on the axis, the table, the hover and the exported provenance line, and the provenance no longer echoes the (inert, hidden) adjusted/original toggle. The number is unchanged: it was never multiplicity-adjusted, and a scan writes it to both p fields because the renderer picks one by pValueType.

Features:
- A dnaMethylation dataset can serve term values on a scale other than the one its matrices store, with queries.dnaMethylation.termValueUnit ('beta' or 'mvalue'); the advertised unit follows. An M-value has no finite value at beta 0, so a build floors those samples near -20 and a plot shows them as a column far from every other value; serving beta puts them at 0. Element classes share the one setting. Differential methylation is unaffected -- it reads the matrices directly, on their stored scale. MMRF now serves beta.
- Survival plot: a Survival control in the plot's settings switches the outcome (e.g. overall to progression-free survival). A plot opened from another chart, such as a DM volcano's per-point or promoter-silencing Survival, starts on the dataset's default survival term and previously could not be switched.
- DM volcano: a "Colour hits at" setting keeps the significance colours only on hits at expressed genes (cohort mean log2 TPM+1 >= 1) or at silent genes (< 0.5) and greys every other dot, in the PNG and the interactive dots alike; the statistics panel adds the up/down split over the highlighted hits. Needs a dataset's dnaMethylation.geneExpressionLevel file.
- DMR scan: the methylome-wide profile (mean delta-beta per bin) under the DMR Manhattan now has its own SVG download, and the two downloads are named for what they hold (DMR Manhattan, methylation profile) instead of manhattan_plot.
- Survival plot: a Filter control in the plot's settings applies a filter to that plot only, on top of the global one. On datasets with several samples per patient it can keep one kind of sample (e.g. MMRF's new Sample collection term, "Baseline · Bone Marrow · CD138pos"), so a patient is not counted once per sample when the curve is split by a sample-level term. A dataset can set termdb.survival.sampleFilter to give every new survival plot that filter by default; MMRF uses it to start every survival plot on diagnostic marrow CD138+ samples.
- DM volcano: a "Promoter silencing" panel lists genes whose normally unmethylated promoter is methylated in a few patients with their expression lost (a dataset's dnaMethylation.silencingScreen file, served by termdb/dmrSilencing), with per-group silenced counts and per-row Survival (split at promoter beta 0.5) and Scatter.
- DM volcano: a "Methylation vs expression" point action opens a region's methylation against its gene's expression as a scatter, one panel per volcano group, with a lowess line. Offered for a region naming one gene, on datasets with expression that enable the summary's scatter (dynamicScatter).
- DM volcano: a "Survival screen" action fits one Cox model per top region (termdb/dmrSurvival, R/src/dmrSurvival.R): methylation per SD, the volcano's covariates, and the two groups as strata, so a prognostic grouping such as t(4;14) is not credited to methylation. Reports HR, 95% CI, p, BH q over the regions screened, the methylation x group interaction p, the HR within each group, and the same model on the region's gene expression. Values enter as rank-based normal scores, so lowly expressed genes' outliers cannot set the hazard ratio, and a log-rank p at each group's own median gives the Kaplan-Meier reading of the same patients. Rows open the per-group Kaplan-Meier plots; hits can be highlighted on the volcano. The screen keeps one sample per patient (the first by name), so a patient with a relapse or blood sample does not enter the model twice. The "Genes, expression and literature" panel runs the same screen on its top concordant genes, filtered to those with PubMed articles on that methylation-expression relationship. The per-point Survival action now opens one plot per group, each split at that group's own median rather than the cohort's, which on a group-shifted region left one arm nearly empty.
- DMR scan blocks can be tested with covariates. utils/dnaMeth/build_block_matrix.py merges a scan export's adjacent same-direction DMRs into domain-scale blocks and builds an element matrix over the CpG shards, so the blocks go through diffMeth.R -- which fits ~ conditions + conf1 + conf2 -- instead of the scan's Rust fit, which has no design matrix and rejects confounders. A dnaMethylation elements[] entry can now set offerWithScanOnly to appear in the class picker on a scanOnly dataset. MMRF gains two such classes, dmr_block (whole span) and dmr_block_called (called intervals only).
- MMRF gains a "Proliferation score (8-gene cell cycle)" dictionary term, built offline from the bulk TPM matrix by ppmmrf/utils/scripts/build_proliferation_score.py and registered at init via termid2sample2value. The cohort ships no proliferation measure of its own, so this is what a differential-methylation or differential-expression contrast can be adjusted by. Covers 378 of the 415 methylation samples; a sample with no value is dropped from the contrast, not imputed.
- DMR scan: scan.permutation={seed} re-runs a scan with the group labels shuffled and group sizes preserved, for an empirical null. Debugmode only. utils/dnaMeth/permutationFdr.mjs drives B draws against a captured diffMeth payload and reports FDR = mean(null)/observed, purging each draw's cache entry. This is the only calibrated region-level FDR a scan can have: DMRcate's region FDR is not calibrated and the background correction's empirical p is floored at 1/(windows+1), so BH over a scan's tests is decided by how many regions were emitted rather than by the evidence.
