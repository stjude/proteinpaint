Features:
- Pathway comparison: from a custom variable of 3 or more groups in the Groups tab, "Compare pathways across groups" runs gene-set enrichment of every group against a chosen reference, on gene expression and on the gene-body methylation of a DMR scan, and shows the results side by side as a bubble heatmap (color NES, size q); rows and columns are sortable and keyboard navigable, and a cell opens that comparison's GSEA plot of the gene set
- GSEA of a DMR scan can rank genes by gene-body methylation change net of expression level ("Net of Expression Level" in the GSEA controls), since gene-body methylation otherwise mostly tracks how expressed a gene is
- The methylation starburst's pathway view runs ActivePathways with directional P-value merging on expression and gene-body methylation (net of expression level by default), shown as a keyboard-navigable heatmap of expression, gene body and the two merged; a gene set's menu rings its genes on the starburst or opens either enrichment plot
- The methylation track shows each sample's isoform share of the gene (its top 5 isoforms and the rest) next to its expression, and its Expression menu offers isoform and promoter usage
- The methylation track's statistics are two rows: mean β of each group with the gap between them shaded by direction, and the correlation with the gene's expression, within the groups when there are groups (each group's ρ pooled), faded where not significant
- The methylation track opened from an expression volcano marks the DMRs of the DNA methylation scan of the same groups, when that scan has been run
- The splice junction track can be drawn as a sashimi plot of two sample groups (first above, second below), offered in its menu when the genome browser has groups and on by default in the methylation view opened from a two-group analysis; an arc's width is the junction's usage in the group, its share of the reads at its busier splice site, so a gene expressed far more in one group still shows which exons each group splices
